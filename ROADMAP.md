# Unpacer: Roadmap & Backlog

Built through real training blocks, travel weeks, and races (`2026 to 2027`).

---

## Live Now (`v0.1`)

- [x] **Plan `X` Weeks Ahead:** Builds your rolling plan from (1) Garmin/Strava history, (2) race + travel calendar, and (3) how recent races actually felt.
- [x] **One-Press Running Workouts:** Open `LAP` warmups + automatic red/green Pace or Heart Rate Zone gauges on Garmin.
- [x] **Multi-Gear Race Pacers:** Terrain-split race workouts (`5 to 7` gears) with `+2 to +3 sec/km` GPS drift built in.
- [x] **Phone-Free Gym on the Wrist:** Animated Garmin Strength workouts (`Free-Gym A`, `Free-Gym B`, `8m Soleus Finisher`) with reps, `kg`, and auto-rest timers.
- [x] **Race-Photo & Shoe Forensics:** Chip-vs-watch split audits from Strava photos, `VDOT` vs. `VO₂max` checks, and late-marathon carbon shoe comparisons.

---

## Next Up (`Backlog`)

### P0: Faster Weekly Flow
- [ ] **Rolling `X`-Week Watch Sync (`plan-weeks`):** Plan `X` weeks in your Master Plan, while auto-pushing only the next `1 to 2` weeks to your watch calendar so travel edits stay effortless.
- [ ] **Paste Coach Text → Watch Workout (`parse-workout`):** Turn any Telegram, WhatsApp, or Final Surge text note (`"WU 20m, 3k-2k-1k-3k @ HMP, CD 15m"`) into a one-press Garmin workout in one step.
- [ ] **Full Garmin 3D Exercise Catalog:** Auto-map any gym exercise to its exact Garmin 3D wrist animation.

### P1: Closed-Loop Coaching
- [ ] **30-Second Post-Run Check-In (`debrief`):** Combine 3 quick feel notes (*legs*, *stomach/caffeine*, *breathing*) with your `1 km` splits to tune the next `X` weeks and update your *Race Playbook*.
- [ ] **Auto Weight Progression (`sync-strength`):** Read completed reps/`kg` from your watch and bump next week's gym targets automatically.
- [ ] **Any `.gpx` Course → Race Pacer (`build-race-pacer`):** Drop in a race `.gpx` + goal time (*Half Marathon `1:24:00`*, *Marathon `2:59:00`*, *Hilly 15K `59:45`*) to get a multi-gear watch workout + morning race card.

### P2: Friends & Apple Watch
- [ ] **Apple Watch Support (`HealthKit` + `WorkoutKit`):** Read Apple Watch running history and export one-press `.workout` files for Apple Watch Ultra / Series users.
- [ ] **5-Minute Friend Setup (`unpacer init`):** Connect Garmin/Strava locally, audit the last `1 to 3` years of runs, and generate a personal runner profile + first `X`-week plan.
- [ ] **`undiary` Connector:** Pull coach messages and post-race voice notes straight from [`undiary`](https://github.com/dnovakovskiy/undiary).
- [ ] **Multi-Sport Cycling + Tendon Guardrail:** Track big summer cycling/bikepacking weeks (`150 to 250+ km/wk`) alongside mandatory `2 to 3` short easy runs/week to protect Achilles stiffness.
